Notes:

  • For Pharmacophore Mapping: Toggle on "Perform GA Match" to use Genetic Algorithm for matching post optimization may increas the computation cost significantly. For the limited mapping accuracy improvement by using GA post optimization comparing with triangle hashing alone, this switch is recommended to be toggled off.
